## Appendix A — Shipping comparison + US cost map

**Read as:** benchmark ranges from public 2026 sources to frame the SEAFRIGO ask — **not quotes.** Cheese ships **refrigerated** (32–40°F, gel packs; dry ice only if transit runs long), with a forgiving 4–6 hr window. DTC shipping typically runs **15–30% of order value**.

| Provider | Type | Direct-to-retail (reefer LTL) | DTC parcel + cold pack | US footprint | Best fit for Monti |

| **SEAFRIGO** (incumbent) | Cold-chain forwarder + 3PL | Yes — domestic trucking + co-pack | Unclear — confirm | NY/NJ port + Miami; global import | Already our import + cold-storage partner; one-stop if they do DTC |
| **RLS Logistics** | Temp-controlled 3PL | Yes | **Yes — DTC fulfillment** (NJ/PA/UT) | East Coast + UT, nationwide transport | Strongest DTC fit; East-Coast like SEAFRIGO |

| **Americold** | #2 cold 3PL (1.3B cu ft) | Yes — national | Limited | National | Retail distribution at scale |
| **FedEx / UPS cold parcel** | Parcel carriers | No (parcel, not freight) | **Yes — the DTC last mile** | National | The actual DTC cost driver; pair with any 3PL |

### US cost map (from a NY/NJ cold-storage origin)

| Region | Reefer LTL — per pallet (retail) | DTC insulated parcel, 5–10 lb, 2-day |
|---|---|---|
| Northeast (local, <300 mi) | $150–$300 | $40–$60 |
| Mid-Atlantic / Southeast (300–1,000 mi) | $400–$800 | $45–$70 |
| Midwest (regional–long) | $400–$900 | $50–$75 |
| South Central / TX (long) | $800–$1,500 | $55–$80 |
| Mountain West (cross-country) | $900–$1,700 | $60–$90* |
| West Coast (cross-country) | $900–$1,800 | $60–$90* |

\* Far zones may exceed 2-day transit → add gel packs or dry ice (transit time, not distance, sets the coolant). Reefer LTL runs ~**$2.56/mi Northeast** up to ~**$3.39/mi South** (national reefer avg ~$3.26/mi, May 2026). DTC parcel = freight ($30–$50 for 2-day, 5–10 lb) + cold packaging ($8–$20). **Serve the Northeast/Mid-Atlantic first — cheapest from NJ.**
