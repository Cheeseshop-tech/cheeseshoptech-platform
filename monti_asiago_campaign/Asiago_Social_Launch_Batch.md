## Light blog post (~500 words)

**Title:** *Product of the Mountains: Why Asiago Tastes of Where It's Made*
**Slug:** product-of-the-mountains-asiago
**Meta description:** Authentic Italian Alpine Asiago DOP from Monti Trentini — made at altitude in the Trentino mountains, milk to wheel, by a family creamery since 1925.

A hundred-year story doesn't happen by chance.

In the Trentino mountains, where cows graze in the shade of the Dolomites, the milk is simply different — and so is the cheese. The herbs of the alpine pasture, the cool air, the slow seasons: they all end up in the wheel. This is what we mean when we call Monti Trentini *Product of the Mountains.* It isn't a slogan. The mountain origin is the product.

Our Asiago DOP is made at 800 metres above sea level, in a certified dairy, from milk sourced within 90 kilometres of our home in Grigno. That short distance matters. Milk that travels less arrives fresher, and milk from one's own neighborhood carries the character of one specific place — not the anonymous average of "Made in Italy."

We also do something increasingly rare: everything happens under one roof. Milk processing, cheesemaking, aging, and packaging all take place in our own plants. Nothing is outsourced; nothing is anonymous. Every wheel is handled start to finish by the people who make it — the way Casa Finco has worked since 1925, four generations of cheesemakers later.

For a cheese counter, that translates into two simple things: a story your customers respond to, and consistency you can build a set around. Our Asiago comes in **whole wheels** for the cut-to-order case and **vacuum-packed quarter wheels** for easy handling and a clean, ready-to-merchandise set. Both are in stock stateside — no import wait.

And there's range to work with. **Fresco** (30–40 days) is mild, milky, and supple — the gateway. **Stagionato** (around five months) turns nutty and firm, the everyday wheel. **Vecchio** (nine months) goes deep and savory, with those little crystalline bites that make a tasting board memorable. One heritage cheese, three expressions for three kinds of shopper.

Making cheese this way is also how we look after the place it comes from. A solar plant generates roughly 650,000 kWh a year — about 80% of our electricity — and avoids close to 1,000 tons of CO₂ annually. Protecting the alpine landscape protects the hundreds of mountain farms within it. When the cheese is made right, the whole mountain benefits.

Happiness has plenty of shapes, and we'd like to put a few of them on your counter.

**If you run a cheese shop or specialty grocery and want to bring authentic Italian Alpine Asiago to your case, we'd love to talk.** Reach us at **Sales@montitrentini-usa.com** — and follow along at **@montitrentini**.

*Monti Trentini — Casa Finco, casari dal 1925. High level of happiness.*
