# Asiago — US shipping comparison + cost map (benchmark, to frame the SEAFRIGO ask)

**Read as:** benchmark ranges from public 2026 sources — **not quotes.** Cheese ships **refrigerated**
(32–40°F, gel packs; dry ice only if transit runs long), with a forgiving 4–6 hr window. DTC shipping
typically runs **15–30% of order value**.

## US cost map (from a NY/NJ cold-storage origin)

| Region | Reefer LTL — per pallet (retail) | DTC insulated parcel, 5–10 lb, 2-day |
|---|---|---|
| Northeast (local, <300 mi) | $150–$300 | $40–$60 |
| Mid-Atlantic / Southeast (300–1,000 mi) | $400–$800 | $45–$70 |
| Midwest (regional–long) | $400–$900 | $50–$75 |
| South Central / TX (long) | $800–$1,500 | $55–$80 |
| Mountain West (cross-country) | $900–$1,700 | $60–$90* |
| West Coast (cross-country) | $900–$1,800 | $60–$90* |

\* Far zones may exceed 2-day transit → add gel packs or dry ice (transit time, not distance, sets the
coolant). Reefer LTL runs ~**$2.56/mi Northeast** up to ~**$3.39/mi South** (national reefer avg
~$3.26/mi, May 2026). DTC parcel = freight ($30–$50 for 2-day, 5–10 lb) + cold packaging ($8–$20).
**Serve the Northeast/Mid-Atlantic first — cheapest from NJ.**

**Sources:** Seafrigo America · Shipfare (DTC perishable shipping) · FedEx (ship perishables) ·
Warp (LTL benchmarks 2026) · Scale Funding (freight rates) · RLS Logistics · Hopstack (top US cold-chain).
