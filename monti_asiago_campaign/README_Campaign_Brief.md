# Monti Trentini — Asiago Launch Campaign

**Audience:** National Cheese Shop Campaign list (HubSpot active list, ~150 cheese shops / boutique groceries — NYC, LA, SF, Chicago)
**Products:** The three Asiago DOP — **Fresco** (30–40 days) · **Stagionato** (5 months) · **Vecchio** (9 months)
**Formats promoted:** **Whole wheels** and **1/4 wheels** (vacuum-packed)
**Stage-1 goal:** **Open a dialogue with the decision-maker** (owner / cheese buyer) and build the relationship — not close an order.
**Pricing & freight:** **By inquiry.** FedEx/UPS is quoted to each shop's location, so price comes *after* the conversation starts. Lead with a taste and a talk, never a number.
**Status:** Inventory already landed and in-stock in the US via **Seafrigo** cold chain — ready to ship, no lead-time wait.

## The one-line story

> **Product of the Mountains.** Authentic Italian Alpine Asiago DOP — milk from within 90 km of the dairy in Grigno, made, aged, and packed in Monti Trentini's own plants at 800 metres. One hundred years of family craft, Casa Finco — *casari dal 1925.*

## The three pillars (every asset leans on these)

1. **Alpine altitude milk** — "Where cows graze in the Dolomites' shades." Made at altitude in the Trentino mountains; the mountain origin *is* the product, not a tagline.
2. **100 years of craft** — Casa Finco, four generations, since 1925. Everything in-house: milk processing → cheesemaking → aging → packaging. Nothing outsourced, nothing anonymous.
3. **Sustainable operation** — solar plant ~650,000 kWh/yr, ~80% electricity self-sufficiency, ~1,000 tons CO₂ avoided annually; protecting the alpine landscape and the mountain farms behind it.

## The Asiago ladder (the merchandising story)

| Cheese | Age | Profile in a sentence | Whole wheel | 1/4 wheel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Asiago Fresco DOP** | 30–40 days | Mild, milky, supple — the gateway Asiago | 28–30 lb (#02005) | vac-pack (#02073) |
| **Asiago Stagionato DOP** | 5 months | Fuller, nutty, firmer — the everyday favorite | 17–19 lb (#03023) | vac-pack (#03010) |
| **Asiago Vecchio DOP** | 9 months | Deep, savory, crystalline — the connoisseur's wheel | 16–18 lb (#03003) | vac-pack (#03014) |

Sell the **ladder**, not just a SKU: a shop can carry one, two, or all three and tell a single Alpine story across the case.
