# Higgsfield — Asiago "Wheel Rotate → Wedge Slide → Present" Motion Prompt

Revised 2026-06-20 after watching the actual HF render (hf_20260620_202456…mp4).

## What the last render did wrong (observed)
- Wheel rotation: good — slow few-degree turn, photoreal.
- Wedge slides out and **lies flat**, cut-face up — fine so far.
- **The tip-up is the failure.** From flat, the wedge pops up abruptly onto its
  rind edge with no visible pivot, no grounding, and no easing — it springs /
  teleports upright instead of hinging on a fixed contact edge.
- Composition drifts: the wheel slides out of frame so the standing wedge ends
  up alone. Lost the "wedge standing *beside* the wheel" relationship.
- Watermark: the "ît" mark top-left is Higgsfield's PLAN watermark — not from
  the prompt. Remove it only via a watermark-free export tier, not prompt text.

## MAIN PROMPT (image-to-video)

A whole wheel of aged Asiago cheese on a soft cream-ivory background. The wheel
slowly rotates a few degrees. A single triangular wedge slides smoothly
outward from the wheel and comes to rest beside it. The wedge then tilts up in
one continuous, graceful motion — hinging on its long curved rind edge where it
contacts the surface, the cut face rotating up and turning to present toward
the camera — until it stands upright and settled on its rind edge, its flat cut
face fully facing the viewer. The pivot edge stays planted on the surface the
entire time: a smooth grounded hinge, gravity-respecting, no popping, no
springing, no sliding, no floating. The wheel stays in frame to the side,
leaving open negative space beside the standing wedge. Motion eases in and out
and settles to a held hero pose.

Photoreal macro food cinematography. Crumbly granular aged Asiago paste with
crystalline grain and pale-straw color, firm natural branded rind, soft
cream-ivory seamless background. Warm studio key light from upper left, soft
contact shadow that stays connected under the cheese, shallow depth of field,
gentle slow camera push-in. Premium artisanal Italian cheese product film.

## MOTION BEATS (use if HF exposes a timeline / keyframes)
- 0.0–2.0s  Wheel rotates a few degrees, ease-in-out.
- 2.0–3.5s  One wedge slides outward, settling beside the wheel, still flat.
- 3.5–6.0s  TILT-UP — pivot on the curved rind edge (the table-contact edge).
            Cut face rotates up toward camera, wedge rises to standing.
            Slow ease-in-out, contact edge fixed, shadow stays attached.
- 6.0–8.0s  Hold upright, cut face to camera; gentle push-in finishes.

## CAMERA / FRAMING
- Keep the wheel in frame beside the wedge — do NOT let it drift out.
- Camera: locked or very slow push-in only. Let the cheese carry the motion.
- Pivot anchor: the wedge's long curved RIND edge resting on the surface — a
  hinge. NOT a mid-air flip, NOT a rear-edge flop, NOT an abrupt pop-up.
- Easing: ease-in-out on every beat. No linear/robotic or springy motion.

## NEGATIVE PROMPT
watermark, logo, "ît" mark, text overlay, captions, abrupt pop-up, springing,
teleporting, ungrounded motion, mid-air flip, backward flop, sliding, floating,
levitating, jitter, wobble, detached shadow, wheel leaving frame, melting,
deforming, rubbery or plastic texture, duplicated wedges, extra cheese, hands,
knife, fast motion, motion-blur smear, warped geometry, morphing edges.
