Four shelf talkers for the Asiago set, themed on authenticity, provenance and origin rather than the sensory-recognition cue explored in the G/H/I round. Same 2.5″ × 3.5″ canvas, same no-photo decision, built on layout G (green cap) — the header logic is swappable if you'd rather land on H or I. Each card carries a mini locator map of the DOP zone, tying the set to the slide below. The slide is a 16:9 recreation of the region map on p.2 of the consortium booklet (design/asiago-booklet/reference-photos/IMG_9789.jpg), redrawn in the Monti Trentini palette. The origin map — one slide, 16:9

Redrawn from the booklet's own map: the shaded zone is where Asiago DOP may be made at all. Cities in cream sit inside the zone; cities in red sit outside it — including Bolzano, which is the point the booklet map makes visually. Enego is added (not in the booklet) because it's where Monti Trentini's own mountain dairy sits, which card 2 depends on.

Denominazione d'Origine Protetta

Asiago can only
be made here.

Not a style. Not a recipe. A place — a geographically defined zone around the Asiago plateau, spanning the province of Trento, the province of Vicenza, and adjoining lowland communes. Milk produced outside these lines cannot become Asiago DOP, whoever makes it.

Blocking — read before this set goes to print

1. The Vecchio card is the one that can't ship yet. An authenticity-themed card is exactly where the open 9-vs-10-month question bites hardest: the whole card argues "the regulator guarantees this," while the consortium's own booklet defines Vecchio as over 10 months and the record ships at 9. I've left the DOP-floor cell reading see note rather than print a number that contradicts the regulator on a card whose subject is regulatory authenticity. This is open item #2 in the handoff — Stefano's answer decides whether the cell reads "10 months," the age changes, or the product gets renamed.

2. Zone boundary needs the disciplinare. The repo's booklet doc describes the zone as "Trento, Vicenza, Belluno and part of Padova." The booklet photo shows Belluno city clearly outside the shading, and the Asiago DOP disciplinare is generally cited as Vicenza + Trento provinces plus parts of Padova and Treviso — no Belluno. I drew Trento + Vicenza solid (unambiguous in the photo) and the adjoining lowland as a dashed "partial" lobe. Confirm against the actual disciplinare before print — a provenance piece that draws the zone wrong is worse than one that doesn't draw it.

3. Make location is asserted only for card 2. signs.json names Caseificio Finco / Enego for the Montagna record only. The other three say "Trentino & Veneto · PDO milkshed," so their cards claim the milkshed and not a dairy. If they're made at Grigno, that's a stronger origin line — but it needs confirming, not assuming.
