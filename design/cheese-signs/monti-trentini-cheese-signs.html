MONTI TRENTINICasari dal 1925 · Grigno, Valsugana

Asiago Fresco

Asiago DOP Fresco

Mild, sweet, milky — soft texture, white to pale straw.

Cow's milkWhole (full-fat)
Trentino & VenetoPDO milkshed
30–40 days
Minimum age

Asiago DOP Fresco is the young face of Italy's most-produced PDO mountain cheese. Monti Trentini makes it with full-fat cow's milk collected daily from farms inside the protected milkshed of Trentino and Veneto — the same valleys the family has bought milk from for four generations. Aged 30 to 40 days, it comes out mild, sweet and clean, with a supple texture and a white to pale straw paste. It slices, melts and cubes well, which makes it the workhorse of the case: as good on a board with fruit as it is in a panino, a gratin or a lasagne. Rich in protein with a well-balanced fat content.

Worth knowingMilk collected only inside the PDO area. Certified organic version available.

Asiago Fresco di Montagna

Asiago DOP Fresco Prodotto della Montagna

Sweet and full-bodied — soft, delicate, high-pasture aromatics.

Cow's milkWhole, above 2,000 ft
Altopiano di Asiago · EnegoCaseificio Finco, Enego
60–70 days
Minimum age

To carry the "Product of the Mountain" mark, every step — the milk, the make, the aging — has to happen above 2,000 feet. This Asiago does. It is produced at Monti Trentini's own Caseificio Finco in Enego, on the Asiago plateau, from full-fat milk rigorously selected from high-pasture herds. The result is what a mountain cheese is supposed to taste like: sweeter and more full-bodied than a valley Asiago, with the flower-and-grass aromatics that come from what the cows actually eat, and a soft, delicate paste. Caseificio Finco took first prize for exactly this cheese at Caseus Veneti 2025.

Worth knowing"Product of the Mountain": milk collected AND processed above 2,000 feet. First prize, Caseus Veneti 2025.

Asiago Stagionato

Asiago DOP Stagionato

Sweet and savory — soft-firm, small scattered eyes, straw-gold paste.

Cow's milkSemi-skimmed
Trentino & VenetoPDO milkshed
5 months
Minimum age

Where the Fresco is a young cheese, the Stagionato is Asiago growing into itself. Monti Trentini makes it from semi-skimmed cow's milk selected in the mountain and valley farms of Trentino and Veneto, then holds it five months — well past the ninety-day PDO minimum. Aging concentrates it: the paste turns straw-gold and firmer, small scattered eyes open through it, and the flavor moves from milky-sweet toward savory and nutty without ever turning sharp. Excellent with nothing but bread, and the Asiago most chefs reach for when they want the cheese to be tasted rather than melted away. High in protein with a well-balanced fat content.

Worth knowingSemi-skimmed milk from small farms; five months of natural aging.

Asiago Vecchio

Asiago DOP Vecchio

Strong, faintly piquant — semi-hard, granular, crystalline.

Cow's milkSemi-skimmed
Trentino & VenetoPDO milkshed
9 months
Minimum age

Nine months of patient natural aging turn Asiago into something else entirely. Made from semi-skimmed milk selected from small farms in Trentino and Veneto, the Vecchio comes out semi-hard and granular, straw-gold, with scattered eyes and the beginnings of crystal crunch. The flavor is direct — deep, savory, faintly piquant on the finish — the kind of cheese that holds its own next to a big red instead of disappearing under it. Serve it in shards with honey, mostarda or aged balsamic; grate it over pasta where you would otherwise reach for something one-dimensional. Rich in protein, well-balanced in fat.

Worth knowingNine months of natural aging; unmistakable personality with a slight spice on the finish.

Truffle Caciotta

Caciotta al Tartufo

Sweet milk against intense, aromatic black truffle.

Cow's milk100% Italian, short chain
Valsugana, TrentinoGrigno (TN)
5 days
Minimum age

A young, soft caciotta veined through with real pieces of Italian black summer truffle — the whole point of this cheese is that the truffle is truffle, not an aroma dosed in at the end. Made in Grigno, in the Valsugana valley, exclusively from 100% Italian cow's milk on a short supply chain, then aged only briefly so the paste stays sweet, supple and milky. That sweetness is what makes it work: it gives the truffle something to push against instead of overwhelming the palate. Cut it into cubes for an aperitivo board, melt it into gnocchi or a risotto, or fold it into a potato krapfen with apple and speck the way the producer's own kitchen does.

Worth knowingReal pieces of Italian black summer truffle — not truffle flavoring.

Mountain Herbs Caciotta

Caciotta alle Erbe (Bigonda)

Fresh and green — alpine herbs over sweet young milk.

Cow's milk100% Italian, short chain
Valsugana, TrentinoGrigno (TN)
5 days
Minimum age

Bigonda takes a young Valsugana caciotta — 100% Italian cow's milk, short supply chain, made and aged in Grigno — and rolls it in a dried selection of herbs gathered from the alpine territory around the dairy. The herbs perfume the rind and scent the paste without ever taking it over, so what you taste first is fresh sweet milk and then the green, faintly resinous lift of the mountain. Soft, easy and immediate: the wedge that gets eaten first on a cheese board. Excellent on brown bread, in a salad with marinated pumpkin and prunes, or simply with a chestnut honey and a glass of something white and alpine.

Worth knowingRolled in a dried selection of herbs gathered from the alpine territory.

Black Pepper Caciotta

Caciotta al Pepe (Pepato)

Sweet milk, then whole peppercorn heat and crunch.

Cow's milk100% Italian, short chain
Valsugana, TrentinoGrigno (TN)
5 days
Minimum age

Whole black peppercorns, not ground pepper — that is the difference you notice. Studded through a young Valsugana caciotta made from 100% Italian cow's milk on a short supply chain, they give the cheese both a warm, aromatic heat and a real crunch as you hit them. Underneath, the paste stays soft, sweet and milky, which keeps the pepper from running away with the cheese. It is the caciotta for people who find a plain young cheese too polite: put it on a board with salumi, shave it over a warm dish so the pepper blooms, or work it into spaghetti with crushed pistachios the way the producer's kitchen does.

Worth knowingWhole black peppercorns in the paste — you feel them, not just taste them.

Imbriago — Drunken Cheese

Imbriago (Ubriaco della Valsugana)

Aromatic and full-bodied — wine on the rind, soft sweet paste inside.

Cow's milk100% Italian, short chain
Valsugana · DolomitiMerlot IGT delle Dolomiti
30 days
Minimum age

The story is the selling line: in hard years, farmers in the Veneto and Trentino hid their cheese in the biggest wine barrels they owned so it could not be seized — and discovered the wine had made it better. Imbriago is that accident, kept on purpose. A young cow's-milk wheel is bathed long and patiently in Merlot IGT delle Dolomiti until the rind takes on a deep wine-purple and the aromatics work their way into the paste. What is left is soft-textured with small sparse holes, full-bodied, faintly winey and unmistakably aromatic. Cut it so the purple rind shows — it sells itself on the board — and pour the same grape alongside.

Worth knowingBathed in Merlot IGT of the Dolomites; the rind is stained wine-purple.

Aged Black Truffle Cheese

Fioretto Stagionato al Tartufo

Concentrated truffle over a firm, faintly granular paste that still melts.

Cow's milkFull-fat, 100% Italian
Valsugana, TrentinoAged on wooden boards
90 days
Minimum age

This is what happens when the truffle caciotta is given time. Made with full-fat 100% Italian cow's milk and real pieces of black summer truffle, then aged at least ninety days on wooden boards, it turns pale gold, firm and compact, with a texture that reads slightly granular right up until it melts on the palate. The aging concentrates everything: the truffle is more intense but stays balanced by the sweetness of the milk, and the cheese gains the savory depth the young version does not have. Shave it rather than cube it. On request the wheel can be finished with an external black coating — a genuinely striking cut on a full-service counter.

Worth knowingNinety days on wooden boards — the aged answer to the young truffle caciotta.

Imbriago (Ubriaco della Valsugana)

Aromatic and full-bodied — wine on the rind, soft sweet paste inside.

The story is the selling line: in hard years, farmers in the Veneto and Trentino hid their cheese in the biggest wine barrels they owned so it could not be seized — and discovered the wine had made it better. Imbriago is that accident, kept on purpose. A young cow's-milk wheel is bathed long and patiently in Merlot IGT delle Dolomiti until the rind takes on a deep wine-purple and the aromatics work their way into the paste. What is left is soft-textured with small sparse holes, full-bodied, faintly winey and unmistakably aromatic. Cut it so the purple rind shows — it sells itself on the board — and pour the same grape alongside.

Worth knowingBathed in Merlot IGT of the Dolomites; the rind is stained wine-purple.

Aged Black Truffle Cheese

Fioretto Stagionato al Tartufo

Concentrated truffle over a firm, faintly granular paste that still melts.

Cow's milkFull-fat, 100% Italian
Valsugana, TrentinoAged on wooden boards
90 days
Minimum age

This is what happens when the truffle caciotta is given time. Made with full-fat 100% Italian cow's milk and real pieces of black summer truffle, then aged at least ninety days on wooden boards, it turns pale gold, firm and compact, with a texture that reads slightly granular right up until it melts on the palate. The aging concentrates everything: the truffle is more intense but stays balanced by the sweetness of the milk, and the cheese gains the savory depth the young version does not have. Shave it rather than cube it. On request the wheel can be finished with an external black coating — a genuinely striking cut on a full-service counter.

Worth knowingNinety days on wooden boards — the aged answer to the young truffle caciotta.
