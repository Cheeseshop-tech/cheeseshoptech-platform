# CheeseShop TECH — Positioning Brief (one page)

**Status:** Draft v1 · 2026-06-16 · **Owner:** Rick Posada · Source for the www landing page + all messaging.

## What we are
CheeseShop TECH is a **full-service, sales-focused brand & growth partner** — we **build, develop, and
maintain** our clients' sites and **run their social, retail, and marketing campaigns** to drive sales. We
are **not** a hosting platform. The platform is the engine; the service is the product.
*(Canonical: CheeseShop TECH = the platform/agency Rick owns; clients like Monti Trentini are tenants.)*

## Who it's for
Small-to-medium companies that want the **outcomes of a large, expensive in-house brand team** but can't
staff one. Owner-led brands with real products and a sales goal, not a vanity-metrics goal.

## The sweet spot (white space we own)
- **Agencies** sell channels & deliverables — fragmented, measured in clicks.
- **Fractional CMOs** sell strategy — but bring no production team.
- **A big in-house team** does it all — but it's expensive and out of reach.
- **CheeseShop TECH** is the rare **full stack**: brand system + a **physical production studio** + the
  **Content Engine wired into the client's CRM** — **owning the outcome (sales)**, at a small-senior-team price.

## Positioning statement
> For owner-led small & mid-size brands, CheeseShop TECH is the brand-to-sales partner that runs your entire
> brand — voice, content, and campaigns — as one engine aimed at sales, giving you a big team's output from a
> small, senior one.

## Messaging pillars
1. **One voice, everywhere it sells.** Brand Kit + Brand Voice continuity across every customer & prospect
   touch. (Strong brand consistency correlates with ~10–20% revenue growth.)
2. **Owns the outcome, not the busywork.** Judged on sales impact — not clicks or impressions.
3. **Full stack, small team.** Strategy + production studio + tech (Content Engine → CRM) under one roof.
4. **Curated, not mass.** A handful of partner brands at a time; senior attention, total focus.

## Platform advantage — the moat (why we can promise big-team output at a small-team price)
Competitors are one or the other; we're both. Pure agencies/fractional shops sell **labor** (so senior
attention doesn't scale affordably); pure SaaS sells **software** (no craft, no service). **CheeseShop TECH is
a platform-powered boutique** — the software does the heavy lifting so a small senior team delivers a big
team's output. The platform IS the economic engine behind the positioning:

- **Single-source Brand Kit + Brand Voice** per client → brand continuity is *enforced by the system*, not
  re-remembered per project. ("One mind, one body.")
- **Content Engine → Content Studio → Content Library**, plugged into the client's **CRM** → compose proposals,
  decks, social, and blog from the brand kit + assets, organized and tied to the sales pipeline.
- **Media Hub (Cloudinary-backed)** → one tagged asset store + dispatch hub; assets reused everywhere, no sprawl;
  light **link + thumbnail** storage instead of heavy files.
- **Multi-tenant + clone-ready** → onboard each new client fast off proven templates (build once, deploy per
  client) → this is *why* the model scales to 10+ clients without ballooning headcount.
- **Owned physical production studio** → vertically integrated photo/video that feeds the Media Hub.
- **House-controlled inputs** → brand governance; clients use the tools, we own the brand system.

**One line:** *the platform multiplies the team* — that's how a few senior people deliver enterprise-grade
brand continuity and sales content for SMB budgets.

## Appendix A — Competitive sampling + psychological levers (2026)
How the field positions, and the lever each pulls:

| Agency | Tier / age | Core message | Psychological lever |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ogilvy | Large, 1948 | "Makes Brands Matter" | Authority + significance |
| Wolff Olins | Large, ~1965 | "Transformative brands that move the world forward" | Ambition + transformation at scale |
| Pentagram | Large independent, 1972 | Partner-led; every client works directly with a partner | **Craft prestige + senior access** |
| COLLINS | Mid independent | Move clients to "better futures" | Aspiration + proof-by-awards |
| Red Antler | Boutique, ~2007 | Launch challenger brands; consumer psychology | **Underdog energy + founder intimacy** |
| Fractional CMO shops | Small/new | "Co-owners of outcomes," "every dollar measurable" | **Outcome ownership + risk reversal** |

**The split we exploit:** big agencies own authority/transformation but no intimacy and no tie to *your sales*;
boutiques own craft + challenger momentum but stop at launch; fractional shops own outcome + trust but bring no
production/craft/system. **No one combines senior partner-level attention + full-stack execution + owning the
sales outcome + curation.** That's the white space.

**Levers to pull (borrowed from the best of each tier):** ① exclusivity/scarcity (invite-only, few brands) ·
② senior intimacy (work with the principals) · ③ risk reversal (judged on sales) · ④ aspirational identity
(big-team brand power) · ⑤ underdog enablement (punch above your weight). The hero copy above already uses
①③④; add ②⑤ in the supporting sections.
